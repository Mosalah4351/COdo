# World Cup 2026 Fantasy

## Summary
A web-based fantasy football game for the FIFA World Cup 2026, held across the USA, Mexico, and Canada (48 teams, 104 matches). Players pick squads, set lineups, trade during windows, and compete on live-scoring leaderboards.

## Vision
Make the most engaging World Cup fantasy experience — fast, social, and mobile-first. Live points update as matches play. Friends leagues and global leaderboards. Works perfectly on phone browsers during matchday.

## Key Differentiators
- Real-time points as matches play (not post-match batch)
- 48-team roster adds strategic depth vs. traditional 32-team formats
- Mobile-first PWA installable from the browser
- Social: friends mini-leagues and trash-talk chat
- Free to play, no pay-to-win mechanics

## Success Metrics
- 10k+ registered users by end of group stage
- 60%+ next-day retention during tournament
- <3s LCP on mobile
- 99.5% uptime during matchdays

## Tech Constraints
- Web-only (no native app)
- Server costs must stay under $200/month at 10k users
- Must support 48 teams and 104 matches (new expanded format)
